# ADR 0089: Hotel deals and promotions

Status: Accepted for the scope built here. Evidence: `docs/audits/2026-10-23-hotel-deals-promotions-audit.md`. Specification: `docs/hotel-deals-promotions-workflow.md`.
Date: 2026-10-23

## Context
ADR 0058 and ADR 0067 F-09 gave a rate plan four kinds of promotion, priced by one evaluator. Four things were missing for a hotel to run promotions safely:
1. No hotel-level object: a promotion lived on one plan, with no code, status, version, scope, audience or approval. A single holder of `supply.rates.manage` could change a live plan's promotions and the new price sold at once (audit finding, `supply.service.ts:697`).
2. On a strict deployment the runtime role may only read `RatePlanPromotion` (ADR 0032), so the existing editors answer a typed 403 there. Verified by `strict-runtime-role-hotel-deals.e2e-spec.ts` SD-05.
3. No commercial protection beyond "a night stays at least one minor unit": no maximum discount, sell floor or margin check, and no way to limit a promotion to a market, an agency or a channel.
4. No place for the screens a revenue manager needs: validate, simulate, submit, approve, activate, pause, expire, history.

## Decisions
1. **A deal is a hotel-level aggregate, priced by the one evaluator.** New tables `HotelDeal` and `HotelDealRatePlan` (migration `202612050001_hotel_deals`). ACTIVE deals are read next to `RatePlanPromotion` rows by one loader (`supply/deal-rules.loader.ts`), mapped to `PromotionRule` by one function (`deal-model.ts` `dealToRule`) and priced by `evaluateContractedStay`. Nothing is compiled into `RatePlanPromotion` (rejected: it would be a privileged write on the strict role and a second copy of the terms). Every pricing surface (Agent search, recheck, hold, Admin readiness, assessment, rate certification, the Sellability Inspector and the simulator) receives deals through the required `StayPlanInput.dealRules` field, so a surface that forgets them fails type-check or throws `RATE_PLAN_DEALS_NOT_LOADED`.
2. **Seven supported types; one refused.** `PERCENT_OFF`, `EARLY_BIRD`, `FLASH_SALE`, `STAY_X_PAY_Y` (priced as `FREE_NIGHTS`), `SUBSEQUENT_NIGHTS`, `EVERY_NTH_FREE`, `FIXED_OFF`. `PACKAGE_ADD_ON` is refused with `PROMOTION_TYPE_UNSUPPORTED`: it changes what is sold, not the price, and the contract model has no priced inclusion. Semantics are in the specification.
3. **Supplier-funded only.** The discount reduces the supplier net and the markup follows it (ADR 0058). Platform or hotel funding changes ledger and settlement treatment and needs a finance decision (D3). `fundingSource` other than `SUPPLIER` is refused by the API and by a database CHECK.
4. **No below-net override exists.** A night never goes below 1 minor unit of net, a markup is never negative, and no field, permission or approval lifts that. A request carrying `allowBelowNet`, `belowNetOverride` or any unknown field is refused (`DEAL_FIELD_NOT_ALLOWED`).
5. **Commercial guards live in the evaluator.** A deal may carry `maxDiscountBp`, `minSellFloorMinor` and `minMarginBp`. They are judged on the nights the deal changed, on the real net plus markup. A breach, or a guard that cannot be verified (a SELL-basis rate under a margin guard, no markup information), means the deal does not apply and the buyer gets the base rate. A stack is held to the guards of every deal in it. A discount above 50% must carry a sell floor.
6. **Selection is unchanged in spirit.** The larger saving wins. `priority` only breaks a tie between equal savings and never overrides a guard or a larger saving. Stacking needs `combinable` on both sides plus no shared `stackingGroup` and no `incompatibleDealIds` entry in either direction. The same deal is applied once.
7. **Audience narrows, never grants.** `channels` (`PORTAL`, `API`), `sourceMarkets` (the buying agency's country) and `agencyIds` are allow-lists that fail closed when the buyer attribute is unknown. Channel is stated by the pricing caller (the Agent adapter says `PORTAL`), never read from a request. Agency segments and member tiers do not exist in the data model and are not offered. The `API` channel is accepted but applies nowhere until a distribution search exists (the Admin says so).
8. **Effective dates are enforced by the evaluator, not by a job.** `activeFrom` and `activeTo` are hotel-calendar days checked in `promotionApplies`. An unknown hotel day fails closed. Stored labels (SCHEDULED to ACTIVE, ACTIVE to EXPIRED) are reconciled by an idempotent, audited `POST /admin/commercial/promotions/reconcile`. A periodic sweeper would need its own narrow database role and is not built (see the runbook). Readers always show what applies today (`effectiveStatus`).
9. **Maker-checker through the existing approval service.** New approval action `promotion.activate` (S3, approval only, refines `supply.rates.manage`, the repository convention for markup, FX, rate plan and contract). Submit validates, fingerprints the canonical terms (SHA-256) and requests approval. A different person decides (`ApprovalService` refuses self-approval). Activation is a separate call that re-checks version, fingerprint, validation and window under a row lock and is single-use. Approval and activation are two recorded decisions with two actors. A dedicated grantable approver permission is a later change (D5).
10. **The database enforces the lifecycle too.** Trigger `fbeds_hotel_deal_guard`: a deal starts as DRAFT, moves only along allowed transitions, and its terms cannot change once it leaves DRAFT; returning to draft raises `version` and clears fingerprint, approval and activation. Trigger `fbeds_hotel_deal_plan_guard`: the scope changes only in DRAFT and a plan must belong to the deal's hotel. CHECK constraints mirror the validator per type. Forced RLS and composite `(tenant_id, id)` foreign keys keep every reference inside the tenant.
11. **Strict runtime role.** `HotelDeal` is read, inserted and updated on an explicit column list (identity, tenant, hotel, creator, idempotency key and `created_at` are not updatable); `HotelDealRatePlan` is read, inserted and deleted; neither is deleted from. Registered in `runtime-role-contract.ts`; the privilege matrix in `docs/strict-runtime-db-role.md` is regenerated; the live catalogue matches (`certify-disposable-runtime-role.cjs`, SD-01).
12. **Mandatory control.** Reading deals is a commercial control (ADR 0031): a denied, failed or malformed read stops search and recheck (`provider_unavailable`, no offer, no price) instead of selling an unchecked price. Admin diagnostic views show the plan's own price on a denied read and say nothing is guessed; rate certification refuses.
13. **Money.** Integer minor units everywhere; contracts carry minor units as decimal strings; percentages are basis points. `FIXED_OFF` applies only in its own currency and never converts.
14. **Audit.** Every mutation writes an `AuditEvent` in its own transaction (`promotion.deal.created|updated|submitted|approved|rejected|activated|paused|resumed|suspended|returned_to_draft|archived|started|expired`) with actor, request id and field-level changes (notes and descriptions are never copied). The approval request keeps requester, decider, decision reason and executor.

## Not decided, not built (invariant 10)
- `PACKAGE_ADD_ON`, agency segments, member tiers, promotion codes redeemed by a guest, platform- or hotel-funded discounts, a below-net override.
- Redemptions, attributed bookings, discount value, revenue and margin reporting: booking tables are not writable by the API runtime role, so no redemption is recorded and none is shown.
- A periodic status sweeper (needs a dedicated role). A dedicated approver permission.
- Distribution to external channels: no connector accepts promotion publication; the API channel is not distributed.
- The legacy per-plan promotion editor is unchanged. It remains an open finding (single actor, privileged on the strict role); deals are the governed path.

## Consequences
- A tenant that wants governed discounts uses deals. Plan promotions keep working where the runtime role allows them.
- Search reads two more tables per request (one indexed query on `HotelDealRatePlan`); no deal means an empty result.
- Deploy order: apply the migration, then re-run `ops:provision-api-runtime-role` (the contract lists the new grants), then deploy the API. Until the grants exist, search answers `provider_unavailable`: that is the control failing closed, by design.

## Rollback
Application: remove `PromotionsModule`; revert the loader and `dealRules` wiring (nothing else reads the tables). Data: deals are not referenced by bookings, so `DROP TABLE "HotelDealRatePlan"; DROP TABLE "HotelDeal";` plus the two functions and three enums listed in the migration header restores the previous schema. Prices revert to the base rate plus any plan promotions the moment deals stop loading.
