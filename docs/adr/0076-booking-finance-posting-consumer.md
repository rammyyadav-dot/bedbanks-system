# ADR 0076: The Finance consumer of `BookingFinanceEvent`

Status: **Built, off by default.** Closes release gate 2 of `docs/booking-module-audit-2026-10-18.md` ("a Finance consumer of `BookingFinanceEvent`: ledger posting, supplier payable creation, under the single-writer ledger rule").

## Finding
An Admin-originated booking records its money facts as `BookingFinanceEvent` rows (ADR 0039, Phase 5, ADR 0072) and nothing moves: no agency account is charged, no supplier payable exists, no revenue can be reported, and an agency's balance is wrong the moment such a booking is confirmed. The Agent flow already posts to the wallet ledger; the Admin flow only wrote an outbox with nothing reading it.

## Decision
1. **A consumer module, `apps/api/src/booking-finance-posting`.** A worker polls `fbeds_finance_posting_due_tenants()` (tenant ids only) and drains each tenant under its RLS context. One transaction per event holds the ledger entries, the payable entry, the posting row and the audit event, so an event is wholly accounted for or not at all.
2. **Single writer is kept, not bent.** Every ledger entry goes through `appendLedgerEntry` and every balance read through `ledger-core` (ADR 0062). `pnpm check:ledger-single-writer` passes unchanged. The booking module's role still cannot see the ledger and the consumer's role cannot write a booking, a booking event, a wallet balance or limit, a funding receipt or a ledger row's contents, so neither side can do the other's job.
3. **A dedicated role, `fbeds_finpost_ops` (group `fbeds_finpost`).** Same pattern as ADR 0070. Column-level read of exactly the event, booking and wallet columns the rules need; read and append `LedgerEntry`; read and append its own two tables and `Cancellation` (plus `UPDATE (refund_minor)` there, nothing else); `UPDATE` on the workflow columns of a posting under review; `UPDATE (updated_at)` on `Wallet` only so it can take the wallet row lock; insert-only `AuditEvent`. `LedgerEntry` has no UPDATE or DELETE grant. The connection is `BOOKING_FINANCE_POSTING_DATABASE_URL` and **never falls back**: unconfigured or ungranted is a typed 503 (`BOOKING_FINANCE_POSTING_UNAVAILABLE`), not a post as a broader principal. (The shared `NarrowRoleDatabase` base would otherwise connect with `DATABASE_URL` when its own URL is unset; this class refuses first, and an e2e test pins it.)
4. **Two new tables** (migration `202612010001_booking_finance_posting`, forced RLS, composite tenant keys, rollback in the header):
   - `BookingFinancePosting`: one row per event. `POSTED` and `SKIPPED` are final and immutable (trigger); `REVIEW_REQUIRED` is the only row that is updated, and only to a final status. Stable reason codes, never free text.
   - `SupplierPayableEntry`: append-only signed movements (`ACCRUAL`, `ADJUSTMENT`, `REVERSAL`) of what the operator owes a supplier for a booking. No payment or settlement state exists here.
   - `BookingFinanceEvent` gains `UNIQUE (tenant_id, id)` for the composite keys. A policy `AuditEvent_finance_posting_system_insert` admits exactly the consumer's two SYSTEM audit actions (the general insert policy refuses SYSTEM rows; this is the same mechanism the hold-expiry, sandbox and rate-plan-expiry roles use).
5. **The books must tie out under the ledger integrity monitor (ADR 0068).** It reads the Agent flow's lifecycle: every charge is HOLD, then RELEASE (`…:settle-release`) and DEBIT of the same amount, all on reference `booking:<id>`, summing to the booking total; a cancelled, charged booking has a `Cancellation` record equal to its REFUND entries. A first draft that posted a lone DEBIT, a separate amendment entry and a refund with no record would each have raised a critical finding. So: a confirmation with no on-request hold posts the whole lifecycle (net effect minus the sell price); a refund also writes or moves the `Cancellation` record (also for a refund of zero); an amendment is never a separate entry (see the table). An e2e test runs the real monitor over what the consumer posted and asserts no critical finding, with a control proving the monitor reports the naive posting.
6. **The Agent flow's ledger keys are reused** (`booking:<id>:authorize`, `…:authorize:release`, `…:authorize:settle-release`, `…:authorize:settle-debit`, `…:cancel-refund[:<total>]`). The wallet's unique `(wallet, idempotency key)` therefore makes a double charge, hold or refund between the two paths impossible, and `CancellationSettlementService` recognises a booking charged here as ledger-charged. A booking the Agent path already charged is `SKIPPED / ALREADY_CHARGED_ON_LEDGER`.

## Accounting rules (`posting-plan.ts`, pure, unit-tested)
| Event | Agency account | Supplier payable |
|---|---|---|
| `CONFIRMED` | HOLD, RELEASE and DEBIT of the sell price; or, after an on-request hold of that same amount, RELEASE and DEBIT. A hold of another amount is reviewed (`HOLD_AMOUNT_DIFFERS`) | `ACCRUAL` of the net |
| `ON_REQUEST_HOLD` | `HOLD` the sell price | none |
| `HOLD_RELEASED` | `RELEASE` the held amount, once | none |
| `CANCELLED`, `PENALTY_DECIDED`, `PENALTY_WAIVED` | `REFUND` of (sell − penalty), as a top-up of what was already refunded, and the `Cancellation` record set to that refund; nothing while the penalty is undetermined; never reduced automatically | `REVERSAL` of the whole accrual only when the penalty is zero |
| `AMENDED` | **No entry.** If the amendment was recorded before the confirmation posted, the confirmation is posted at the amended totals and the amendment is `SKIPPED / AMENDMENT_ALREADY_REFLECTED`. If the booking was already charged at another total it is `REVIEW_REQUIRED / AMENDMENT_NEEDS_LEDGER_ADJUSTMENT` and later events of the booking wait | follows the confirmation |

Pay-at-hotel bookings move no money through the operator and are `SKIPPED`. All amounts are integer minor units in the event's currency; nothing is converted or added across currencies.

## Ordering and failure
A booking's events post in the order they happened. If an earlier event needs review the later ones wait (a refund must never outrun the charge it refunds). A review is retried after five minutes. A review never writes a partial posting. A ledger key that already exists but that the facts did not show (a race with the Agent path) becomes a review, not a crash loop. Bookings are serialized by a per-booking advisory lock; the wallet is locked with the same advisory key and row lock, in the same order, as the Agent account paths.

## Owner decisions taken as defaults (change them in `posting-plan.ts` and this ADR)
1. **An agency with no open account is reviewed; the consumer never opens one.** Opening an account is a funding decision (ADR 0028).
2. **The charge posts even if it takes the account over its credit line.** The booking already happened with the supplier; refusing to record it would hide the debt. The ledger integrity monitor reports an overdrawn account. The credit line is not readable by this role.
3. **Penalty-free cancellation reverses the supplier accrual; a cancellation with a penalty keeps it** and notes `SUPPLIER_LEG_AWAITS_STATEMENT`. The event's penalty is the sell-side penalty; the supplier's own fee is unknown until its statement is reconciled (ADR 0070).
4. **An event that did not record its payment mode or net uses the booking's current value;** if that is also unknown the event stays in review until Finance completes the booking.
5. **A confirmation with an unknown net posts nothing** (it does not charge the agency and skip the payable): partial postings would leave no way to complete the payable later.

## Not done
- **Backlog screen (built):** `GET /admin/finance/booking-postings` (`finance.read`, read-only, contract in `@bedbanks/contracts`) and Finance → Booking postings. Complete counts, the oldest 100 events that need a person with a plain-language reason and next step, the oldest 100 not yet attempted, and why events were skipped. It reads through the consumer's own role; when that role is unavailable the page shows a failure, never an empty backlog. It has no actions: resolving a review means fixing the booking or the agency account, and the retry does the rest. The booking finance panel still says "awaiting Finance booking" for every event; changing that wording is not done.
- **Supplier payment/settlement** (paying a supplier, aging of payables) and **the switch of `GET /admin/operations/supplier-payables`** from the booking-net accrual to these entries. Until then that endpoint and the entries can differ; the accrual endpoint is unchanged.
- **Revenue report (built):** `GET /admin/finance/booking-postings/revenue?from&to` (`finance.read`) and Finance → Booking revenue. Per currency: sales (the DEBIT legs), refunds, net revenue, supplier cost (accruals, adjustments and reversals), indicative margin, and the same by agency and by supplier. Migration `202612020001` adds `sales_minor` and `refunds_minor` to `BookingFinancePosting` so the report is a plain aggregation of what the consumer posted: it sums nothing from the ledger and converts or adds no currencies. **Basis and scope are decisions to confirm with Finance:** it is dated by when the event was *posted* (not booking, stay or payment date), it covers Admin-originated bookings only (Agent-flow charges and their supplier cost are not in it), and margin is indicative because a cancelled booking with a penalty keeps its payable until the supplier's own fee is known. Stay-date (accrual) recognition and an Agent-flow-inclusive view are not built.
- **Ledger adjustment for an amendment of an already-charged booking.** ADR 0072 refuses the approval for the same reason (the integrity monitor ties one charge to the booking total, and a charge cannot be reduced). The only amendments that reach the consumer are the ones approved in the lag before the confirmation posted, which are folded in. A review row from a true race has no resolution tool yet: it stays visible and blocks the booking's later events until Finance decides how adjustments are booked (the open question in ADR 0028 and ADR 0054).
- **High-water mark for the due-tenants function.** It anti-joins events to postings; fine at current volume, revisit when the event table is large.

## Verification
Planner unit tests (40). PostgreSQL e2e `booking-finance-posting` (21): role certification and all privilege probes, charge and payable, idempotency under concurrent drains, hold/release/confirm, Agent-flow no-double-charge, pay-at-hotel, cancellation refund, waiver top-up and the `Cancellation` record, penalty-free reversal, full penalty, undetermined penalty, amendment folded in and amendment after the charge, ordering, the ledger integrity monitor reporting no critical finding (with a control), the revenue report tying to the ledger and the payable entries, missing account, unknown net, tenant isolation, immutability, the no-fallback 503, and the existing `CancellationSettlementService` posting nothing extra. Also green: `cancellation-settlement`, `booking-finance`, `booking-amendment`, `booking-cancellation`, `finance-reconciliation-role`, `ledger-*`, `api-runtime-role`, `security-certification` e2e; the full API unit suite; `pnpm check:architecture`; API type-check and lint.

Not run: the browser, CI, a production-shaped database. Run the e2e set in CI before release.
